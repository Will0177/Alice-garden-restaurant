Our Story

A sanctuary of taste and beauty in the heart of Lagos.

The Enchanted Garden

Alice Garden Restaurant was born from a desire to create a dining experience that transcends the ordinary. Located at 57 Oduduwa Way, Ikeja GRA, we have cultivated a space where nature meets culinary excellence.

Our interiors are a labor of love, adorned with cascading flowers and lush greenery, creating a whimsical atmosphere perfect for escaping the city's hustle. It is not just a restaurant; it is a destination for those who seek beauty in every moment.
