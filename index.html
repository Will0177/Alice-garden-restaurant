Nestled in the heart of Ikeja GRA, Alice Garden Restaurant is more than just a dining venue; it's an enchanting escape.

Known for our picturesque, flower-filled decor and serene ambiance, we offer a unique blend of African, Continental, and Asian cuisines. Whether it's a romantic date, a bridal shower, or a casual lunch, every corner is designed to be a visual and culinary delight.
